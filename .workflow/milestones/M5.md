# M5 — Integrate the fixed churn model and experiment lineage

**Goal:** use the existing `lumen-temporal-catboost` 1.0.0 artifact as-is, prepare an A/B test draft, and demonstrate assignment, exposure, renewal outcome and offer-policy learning with simulated events and prediction/model lineage. This project does not train, tune, recalibrate, extend or replace the model. No treatment-uplift model is claimed.

**Dependencies:** M4 handoff, fixed-artifact verification and Lead-frozen versioned interfaces. Live Adapty activation and real-customer A/B tests are outside this demo milestone. Do not dispatch blocked workers before their dependencies are met.

**Data boundary:** no new customer or live A/B results are expected for this demo. The fixed model is checked with its existing fixture and synthetic snapshots, independently of A/B events. Deterministic simulated assignment, exposure and outcome events serve only the experiment-lineage and illustrative offer-policy demonstration; they never train or validate the churn model.

**Pre-model infrastructure wave:** M5-GR-004 restores the previously integrated and reverted M5-GR-001 dashboard; then M5-GR-003 adds descriptive prediction and scenario views. M5-QA-002 independently builds the unit/contract, PostgreSQL/Grafana and fresh Compose test triangle from `docs/PREMODEL_METRICS.md`. These tasks need no external model or live Adapty writes. Record an exact-SHA checkpoint, but do not mark M5 DONE or promote it until the full dependencies and acceptance below are met.

**Offline data wave:** M5-LD-002 freezes `docs/CURRENT_DASHBOARDS.md`; M5-AN-003 imports the local synthetic users and transactions; M5-GR-005 provisions four source-honest dashboards; M5-QA-003 checks the exact SHA. Do not integrate older prediction/mock dashboard work as evidence for this wave. This checkpoint also does not mark M5 DONE. M5-LD-003 tracks model-dependent metrics, verified Adapty exports and the blind analyst comparison after the model arrives.

**Fixed model wave:** `origin/main` commits `7475a89` and `7dc9a96` supply a substantial existing implementation and the unchanged CatBoost artifact `lumen-temporal-catboost` 1.0.0. M5-ML-001 confirms its documented target and safe-use limits before activation, without changing weights or features. M5-LD-001 freezes model I/O and event sources; M5-LD-004 integrates those exact commits into `develop` before implementation jobs begin. M5-BE-001 serves this exact artifact; M5-BE-004 prepares cutoff-safe inputs. M5-AN-005 migrates prediction storage and M5-GR-010 displays trajectories; M5-QA-004 gates their integration. The replay script is a synthetic demonstration, not a production feature feed.

**Demo wave:** M5-QA-005 implements `docs/M5_DEMO.md` after model and event ingestion are ready. Synthetic A/B events show prediction-to-outcome lineage and an isolated example of updating a demo offer policy. No live Adapty writes, churn-model changes or uplift claims. Newer `origin/main` commits `272fc92`, `425dbcb` and `3363f70` are research/optional usage work; they do not change the pinned artifact and are not prerequisites for this demo.

**Acceptance:** predictions from the pinned artifact are distinguishable from mock; the read-only Grafana dashboard displays model outputs and clearly labeled simulated lineage; a reviewed risk cohort can produce a locally validated draft mapping without a live Adapty write; simulated assignments, exposures and outcomes are joinable and idempotent; audit exports exclude post-decision features and unobserved outcomes. No churn-model update or live customer A/B test starts in the gate.

**Demo release boundary:** M5 may be promoted when this synthetic acceptance passes on one exact `develop` SHA. The operational parts of M5-BE-004, M5-BE-002 and M5-AN-002 remain partial because dated real feature sources, verified tenant content and consent evidence are absent. They are not production capabilities or prerequisites for the synthetic M5 demo. Keep their jobs open for the M6 operational wave; do not treat the demo gate as real-customer approval.
