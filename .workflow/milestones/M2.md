# M2 — Break-even & Budget-limited Experiment Planner

**Goal:** backend computes algebraic break-even renewal threshold for 10/20/30% discounts, expected baseline next-renewal revenue at risk, randomized control + offer arm allocations under maximum face-value budget.

**Parallel:** M2-BE-001 + M2-QA-001.
**Acceptance:** `p_min=p/(1-d)`, flag infeasible if >1; no churn model or treatment uplift estimation; sum worst-case discounts <= budget; seeded deterministic; no extra users, all assigned once; no claims of optimized savings.
