# M7 — Cohort Discount Advisor and Adapty draft handoff

**Goal:** integrate the synthetic-data Discount Advisor from `origin/model-implementation`
as a separate cohort scenario calculation, persist versioned reports, display them in
Grafana, and generate reviewable Adapty flow A/B draft manifests where compatible
control and discount flows exist. It is not the external churn model for M5/M6.

**Dependencies:** M4 handoff; Lead-frozen advisor I/O contract; synthetic offline
CSV source for reproducible calculations. Live Adapty deployment additionally
depends on a published discount treatment flow, matching placement/audience,
documented product pricing, and operator approval. M5 churn serving does not
depend on M7.

**Acceptance:** same input, source version and dataset digest yield the same
advisor numbers; reports retain DATA/CALCULATED/ASSUMED provenance; Grafana
shows advisor scenario numbers separately from churn predictions and observed
outcomes; a draft manifest has verifiable flow/product IDs and no secret; a
missing treatment flow produces `not_ready` rather than a fabricated test.
No live placement is changed by the M7 gate.
