# M6-ML-001: Validate, approve and freeze production weights

External ML owner and Product define measurable next-renewal validation criteria before evaluating candidate versions. Review calibration, cohort/time split, leakage, drift and limitations; record approval, immutable artifact/version/checksum and rollback target. Continue PoC training only in the external service; production serves the frozen version until a new explicit approval. Acceptance: later candidate updates do not alter frozen predictions, and a previously approved version can be restored. No treatment-effect claim is inferred from churn-risk validation.
