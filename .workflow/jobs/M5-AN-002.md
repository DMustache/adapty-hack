# M5-AN-002: Export cutoff-safe experiment audit records

Provide a versioned, access-controlled audit export for approved experiment analysis. Include only eligible, consented pseudonymous records with decision-time features, pinned model version, assignment/exposure metadata and observed next-renewal outcome after its horizon closes. Exclude post-decision features, unobserved outcomes and duplicate events; report counts of excluded/incomplete records. This export does not trigger or authorize model training or weight updates, and it is not itself evidence of uplift. Acceptance: sample lineage can be reproduced from immutable source rows, and a test with a future feature or missing outcome is excluded with a reason.
